    03 · Pipelines

    Pipelines for the places games are set in

    A downtown block, a residential street, a landmark, a waterfront. Same graph, different weak points. Each one below is a real run, shown as it shipped.

    A downtown block you can walk

    Place→Footprints→LiDAR heights→Ground→Build→Texture→Walk test→Street judge→glTF

    Best downtown run: Times Square, 24 Oct

    20 / 20routes walked
    105buildings, 90% at LiDAR height
    31 minfor the run
    $4.78model compute (not a price)

    Where it misses: Times Square has none of its signage or LED billboards, only uniform glass towers. The street judge matched at most 1 of 4 buildings to held-out street photos in any of the four runs of 24 October.

    A residential street at eye level

    Place→Footprints→LiDAR heights→Ground→Build + street kit→Texture→Walk test→Street judge→glTF

    17 / 20routes walked (73% over 60)
    2 / 15street photos with matching buildings
    2 / 5street rated a layout blockout

    Where it misses: brownstones come out as generic brick blocks and the parked cars are grey boxes. The judge is uncalibrated, and we state its numbers as it gives them.

    A landmark that has to read at a glance

    Place (by hand)→Footprints (by hand)→LiDAR heights→Ground→Build→Texture→Walk test→Street judge→glTF

    1.5 / 4building match, 2 usable photos
    30%routes walked (target 95%)
    8structures
    132 MBworld file, compressed

    Where it misses: a person ran the first two stages, so this is not a full agent run. The turret and wings read; the palms came out as conifers.

    A working waterfront

    Place→Footprints→LiDAR heights→Ground + water→Build→Texture→Walk test→Street judge→glTF

    18 / 20routes walked
    6structures built (target 100)
    0cranes, ships or containers

    The miss, shown on purpose: ships, containers and cranes have no footprint, so they stay pixels in the ground photo. Ports need a node this graph doesn’t have yet.

    04 · Places

    Thirteen places, one run each

    No hand-picking. Every place we’ve run, with its walk-test score: routes completed out of 20 in our own walk-through test.

      Runs took 15 to 32 minutes and $3.62 to $10.53 of model compute. Without clipping LiDAR heights to building footprints, none of 20 routes complete. Seattle’s LiDAR was missing from our usual catalogue; the agents found the USGS dataset themselves. Sources for every place: NAIP aerial imagery and USGS 3DEP LiDAR (public domain), Overture Maps footprints (ODbL).

      06 · Into your engine

      Runs inside your tools

      One self-contained GLB: terrain, buildings and textures in metres, with the origin at the corner of the place. No plugin, no streaming, no account.

      • UnrealImport the GLB through Interchange.
      • UnityImport it with the glTFast package.
      • GodotOpens natively as a scene.
      • BlenderFile › Import › glTF 2.0. It’s what the agents export from.
