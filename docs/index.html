Real places, ready for your game

Today a real place is modelled by hand from photos, extruded from map data into untextured boxes, or streamed from 3D tiles whose terms forbid keeping them.

You can have a US location built as one glTF from public aerial imagery and LiDAR: measured heights, the aerial photo on the roofs, and a file you can keep and build on. We do this with agents.

Job 1 of 7

Find what’s there

The map doesn’t tell you what’s there. Map data only holds what someone tagged, so whole areas come in with streets and no buildings.

What people do today

  • A Roblox developer building Tokyo traces roads from Google Earth screenshots and places buildings “completely eyeballed”, because exporting the building data was “too tedious”. Roblox devforum
  • A buyer of a $125 Unity city generator: “rural or lesser-known areas often have almost no building data at all”. Unity Asset Store review
  • Players of a game set in real cities find maps with “just streets and few buildings”. The answer they get is to edit OpenStreetMap and wait. Steam discussion

Job 2 of 7

Get real heights

From above you only see roofs. Heights, walls and overhangs have to come from somewhere. Open map data has heights for most buildings in many US cities and for few in others.

What people do today

  • In Overture’s open building data for 13 US neighbourhoods we sampled, 11 have heights for 76–99% of buildings; downtown Kansas City has 36% and downtown Pittsburgh 12%. Where the tag is missing, tools built on map data give every building the same height. Where it is present it can be a placeholder: in a block of houses we built in Austin, 80% of Overture heights sit at 4–6 m and they don’t track the LiDAR roof heights (correlation 0.03 over 106 buildings). Overture, October 2026 · FOSS4G 2023
  • Heights estimated from imagery are loose. A USGS and Ohio State comparison against heights derived from LiDAR at two US sites: Microsoft’s building heights estimated from imagery agree at R² 0.54–0.60 (typical error 2.5–3.1 m); USA Structures, based on LiDAR, at 0.86–0.87 (1.8–2.2 m). CaGIS 2026 abstract
  • A Houdini user extruding San Francisco from map data got buildings all of one height. The thread is unanswered. SideFX forum, 2021
  • Esri’s CityEngine builds city models from OpenStreetMap, but its own FAQ says it “does not support lidar data”; heights from lidar need a separate ArcGIS Pro workflow. CityEngine FAQ
  • A studio rebuilding a Tokyo station measured by phone and from memory, and found the remembered measurements wrong. They aim for 5–10 cm; errors over 20 cm show. Digital Production, 2026

Job 3 of 7

Build it so you can keep it

The data that looks best can’t be kept. Photoreal 3D tiles look right but can’t be turned into game assets, so serious work goes back to modelling by hand.

What people do today

  • A glider sim developer asked to export Google’s 3D tiles as models. Cesium staff: not allowed under Google’s or Bing’s terms. Cesium forum, 2024
  • Under a launch of San Francisco as a game that drew 610 points on Hacker News, a professional said the same terms blocked their own project, and others asked “Is there a guide out there to do this for any area?” Hacker News, 2026
  • A professional XR studio built a real station about 90% by hand in Blender, preferring that to scans whose topology it couldn’t edit. Digital Production, 2026

Job 4 of 7

Make it walkable

Players fall through it. A city that looks right still has to be walkable, collide and run, and generated or streamed geometry often isn’t.

What people do today

  • Characters float above streamed 3D tiles; an aircraft sinks through buildings set to block everything. Unresolved across two years of threads. Cesium forum
  • Buyers of a Unity city generator report navigation that “did not work” and roads “realy bad at usa highways and over hangs”. Unity Asset Store reviews
  • Commenters on the San Francisco web game got stuck in terrain and hit 2 GB of memory and freezes. Hacker News, 2026
  • A modder who built their hometown for BeamNG from LiDAR as glTF made about 95% of the buildings by hand and shipped them without collision meshes or LODs; a player reports 20 fps looking at them. BeamNG forum, 2026

Job 5 of 7

Texture the white boxes

Shapes arrive as white boxes. Measured buildings come without surfaces, and textures are the part people pay extra for.

What people do today

  • Cesium staff, asked for textured buildings for a real place: “OpenStreetMap does not provide textures”. Cesium forum, 2025
  • An archviz user’s generated city is “white blocks” and they want the satellite photo on them. D5 Render forum, 2025
  • Blosm, the Blender map importer (2.1k stars), charges for one thing: textures, trees and imagery on terrain. GitHub
  • A Unity developer fixes “flat-colored boxes” by painting a window texture on at runtime. Developer blog

Job 6 of 7

Take one day’s sun out of the photo

The photo has one day’s sun baked in. Put an aerial photo on a building and its shadows stay, whatever the game’s lighting.

What people do today

  • Production delighting tools assume the capture avoided hard sun. Unity’s own guide calls strong directional shadows “a situation that must be avoided”, and falls back to Photoshop “to remove lighting artistically”. Unity Photogrammetry Workflow
  • Metashape’s remove lighting tool: “delighting in strong shadows is not supported.” Aerial imagery is flown in sunlight, so hard shadows are always there. Agisoft

Job 7 of 7

Get it into your engine

Many hops into the engine. Even with the data in hand, getting a real place into Unreal or Unity is a chain of tools and hand fixes.

What people do today

  • “It’s a LOT of manual work”: a developer making 1:1 real cities in Unreal describes a hop through five tools and terrain data off by 20–30 m. Unreal forum, 2020
  • A Houdini user building city blocks from OpenStreetMap plus a terrain heightfield has to match the two by hand. SideFX staff: “We don’t currently have any other tools that can do that aligning automatically.” Another user fetched heights from QGIS and aligned them themselves. SideFX forum, 2026
  • Georeferenced data in Unreal: no native projection support, format hops, alignment issues. ISPRS, 2025
  • A solo WW2 shooter developer imports real heights, imagery and streets, then finishes roads, ground, structures and set dressing by hand, and wants to “explore how to maybe automate”. itch.io devlog
